Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy explains how cancellations and refund requests are handled for services requested through StudentEase.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. StudentEase Service Fees

StudentEase service charges may vary depending on the type, complexity and time required for a requested service. The applicable StudentEase fee will be communicated before you confirm your request.

Official university, college, government, examination or portal fees are separate unless explicitly stated otherwise. These official fees may be subject to the refund rules of the relevant institution or service provider.

2. Cancellation Before Work Begins

If you cancel a request before StudentEase has started work, you may request a refund of the StudentEase service fee already paid. Any refund will be assessed fairly under the circumstances and applicable law.

3. Cancellation After Work Begins

If work has already started, the refundable amount may depend on the work completed, any clearly disclosed costs already incurred, and the applicable law. We will explain the basis of any deduction rather than automatically treating every payment as non-refundable.

4. Service Not Completed

If StudentEase cannot provide an agreed service, we will review the circumstances and communicate an appropriate resolution. This may include a full or partial refund for the affected service, depending on the work completed and applicable law.

5. Incorrect or Incomplete Information

Students are responsible for providing accurate information and genuine documents. If incorrect or incomplete information delays or prevents a service, we will explain the issue and any available options. Additional work or charges must be communicated before proceeding.

6. Official Fees and Third-Party Payments

Payments made directly to a university, college, government department or third-party portal are governed by that recipient's applicable rules. StudentEase cannot promise a refund of a fee controlled by another organization.

If StudentEase collects an official fee on your behalf, we will clarify the payment details and any applicable refund process. Any amount recoverable from the relevant recipient will be handled according to the applicable rules and circumstances.

7. Duplicate or Incorrect Payments

If you believe you made a duplicate or incorrect payment to StudentEase, contact us with the transaction reference and a description of the issue. We will review the records and arrange any refund that is due.

8. How to Request a Refund

Contact StudentEase using the contact details published on our website. Include your name, the service requested, the payment date, the transaction reference if available, and the reason for your request. Avoid sending passwords, OTPs or unnecessary sensitive information.

We will review the request and communicate the decision and any applicable next steps. If a refund is approved, the expected processing time and refund method will be communicated to you.

9. Processing Times

Refund processing times can vary depending on the payment method, payment provider and any third-party institution involved. We will provide an estimated timeframe when the refund is approved, where possible.

10. Legal Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove or restrict any consumer right or other protection that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services or legal obligations change. The revised version will display an updated date. Changes will apply subject to applicable law and any required notice.

12. Contact Us

For cancellation or refund questions, contact StudentEase using the contact details published on our website.
