Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to StudentEase. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use our website and services.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Introduction

StudentEase provides student-support services, including assistance with forms, applications, documents, certificates, notices and online processes. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you use our website or request our services.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the service you request, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and contact details, such as phone number or email address.
  • Educational details, including college, course, session and semester.
  • Information and documents needed to assist with an application or service.
  • Service requests, payment status and relevant transaction references.
  • Information your browser or device makes available when you visit the website.

We aim to collect only information reasonably needed for the relevant service or a legitimate operational purpose.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide requested assistance.
  • Help prepare, organize or submit applications where authorized.
  • Communicate about service progress, fees and missing information.
  • Maintain service records and resolve payment or support issues.
  • Improve website functionality, reliability and student experience.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and protect against misuse or fraud.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell students' personal information. Information may be shared when necessary to provide a requested service, including with an institution, official portal or service provider, where appropriate and authorized.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to address fraud, security issues or legal claims. We will seek to limit sharing to what is necessary for the relevant purpose.

5. Official Portals and Third-Party Services

StudentEase may link to university websites, scholarship portals, payment providers and other external services. Those services may collect and process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

Please check the relevant official website before submitting personal information or making a payment. StudentEase does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party services.

6. Documents, Passwords and OTPs

Please provide only genuine documents and information needed for your requested service. Do not send passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), PINs or complete payment-card credentials through ordinary chat messages.

If a process requires you to sign in or verify your identity, you should normally complete that sensitive step yourself through the official portal. Any assistance involving account access must be handled only through an appropriate, authorized and secure process.

7. Payments and Financial Information

StudentEase may keep records of service charges, payment status and transaction references needed to manage a request. Where payment is handled by a third-party payment provider, that provider may process financial information under its own terms and privacy policy.

StudentEase service charges and official institution or government fees are separate unless clearly stated otherwise. Check the applicable fee details before confirming a service.

8. Data Storage and Security

We intend to use reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure. Access should be limited to people and service providers who need the information for an appropriate purpose.

No website, online service or electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. We will not claim security measures that have not actually been implemented.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the requested service, record-keeping, dispute resolution or legal requirements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the service involved.

You may contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of your information. Some records may need to be retained where required or permitted by applicable law.

10. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning access to, correction of, deletion of or other actions relating to your personal information. You may also ask questions about how your information is handled or raise a privacy concern.

We will handle such requests in accordance with applicable law and may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

11. Children's Privacy

StudentEase is intended to provide student-support services. Where a student is legally required to have a parent or guardian involved, the appropriate involvement and permissions must be obtained before processing their information or providing the relevant service.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services or legal obligations change. The revised version will show an updated date. Where required, we will provide notice or obtain consent before applying changes.

13. Contact Us

If you have a question, privacy request or concern about the handling of your information, contact StudentEase using the contact details published on our website.
